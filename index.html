Springtime Love: Dating Sim — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game "Springtime Love: Dating Sim" (package name com.gamepsh.datingsim, "the Game") handles information. The Game is published by Three Perk.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment information. Your game progress is saved only on your own device.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about how you interact with ads in order to show ads, limit ad frequency, measure performance and prevent fraud. This data is collected and handled by those providers under their own privacy policies; see policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partners.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Device and diagnostic data

The Game may process basic technical information (device model, Android version, crash information) that Google Play and the advertising service provide to help keep the Game stable. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

4. Children

The Game contains romance themes and simulated gambling and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the applicable minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information handled by advertising providers is retained according to their policies. Deleting the Game removes the locally stored save data from your device.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact shown on the Game's Google Play store page.
